How it works

Three steps, one record.

01

Tell it how you feel

Tap a few moods, type a sentence, pick a colour and the weather inside you — even drop a photo of the moment. However the evening sits with you, MoodTune is listening.

02

An AI DJ reads the room

Not a chart-chasing algorithm — a DJ that actually listens, then reaches for three songs across any genre, mainstream or obscure, chosen for this exact moment and nothing else.

03

Press play, then look back

A hand-drawn vinyl spins your set, its edge rippling as it turns. Every mood you log is pressed onto a calendar — revisit any night, or replay a whole month.
